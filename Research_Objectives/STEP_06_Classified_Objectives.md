# Step 6: Classified Research Objectives

The 8 candidate objectives from Step 5 are classified into Primary and Secondary categories based on their centrality to the research project's core purpose and scope.

## A. Primary Objectives
*These represent the core focus and fundamental goals of the research project.*

**Objective 2: To compare the efficacy of distinct pre-trained CNN architectures...**
- **Why:** The fundamental technical task of this project is multi-class classification of skin lesions. Comparing different transfer learning models establishes the baseline and primary outcomes of the study.

**Objective 3: To investigate the effectiveness of targeted data augmentation strategies...**
- **Why:** The HAM10000 dataset has a notorious class imbalance (~67% nevi). Any reliable classification attempt must fundamentally address this issue; therefore, handling data imbalance is a primary technical challenge.

**Objective 5: To assess model performance systematically using a comprehensive suite of evaluation metrics...**
- **Why:** In medical image analysis, accuracy alone is misleading (especially with imbalanced data). A comprehensive evaluation methodology is critical to the validity of the entire research output.

## B. Secondary Objectives
*These are supportive, analytical, or exploratory goals that enhance the primary findings but are not the absolute core functional requirement.*

**Objective 1: To evaluate the impact of standardized image preprocessing pipelines...**
- **Why:** While important for optimization, preprocessing is a preparatory step. The core research can still function (albeit potentially less accurately) with basic standard preprocessing.

**Objective 4: To implement Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM)...**
- **Why:** Explainability is an advanced, highly valuable feature that sets the project apart, but the primary goal is achieving accurate classification first. It is an excellent secondary/value-add objective.

**Objective 6: To develop a simple ensemble model...**
- **Why:** Ensemble methods are an optimization step taken after individual models have been evaluated. It builds upon the primary objective rather than being the baseline requirement.

**Objective 7: To analyze the specific misclassifications and feature confusion...**
- **Why:** This is a deep-dive analysis into the results generated by the primary objectives. It represents the "discussion" and "analysis" phase rather than the core technical development phase.

**Objective 8: To determine the optimal hyperparameter configurations...**
- **Why:** Hyperparameter tuning is standard practice in ML pipelines. While necessary, it is an operational step rather than a novel research objective in itself.
