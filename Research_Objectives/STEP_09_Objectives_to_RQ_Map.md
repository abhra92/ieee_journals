The following table demonstrates the precise alignment between the proposed research objectives, research questions, experimental design, and evaluation criteria. Every research question corresponds directly to a measurable research objective.

| **Objective 1:** To implement and evaluate image preprocessing techniques—including image resizing, normalization, and artifact suppression—on HAM10000 dermoscopic images and assess their impact on downstream classification accuracy. | **RQ1:** How do image preprocessing techniques (resizing, normalization, artifact removal) affect the classification accuracy of deep learning models on HAM10000? | Compare baseline CNN models trained on raw images vs. images processed with DullRazor (artifact removal) and CLAHE (contrast enhancement). | HAM10000 | Accuracy, Loss | Preprocessed images will yield statistically significant improvements in model accuracy and convergence speed compared to raw images. |
