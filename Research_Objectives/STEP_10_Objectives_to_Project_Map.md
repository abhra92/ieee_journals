# Step 10: Objectives to Project Execution Flow

This table outlines the practical execution strategy for each research objective, ensuring that they are realistic and achievable within the scope of a BCA-level academic research prototype.

| Objective | Method | Dataset | Model | Experiment | Evaluation Metric | Expected Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Objective 1:** Implement & evaluate preprocessing techniques | Apply hair removal (DullRazor algorithm) and contrast enhancement (CLAHE); normalize pixel values to [0,1]. | HAM10000 (10,015 images) | Baseline CNN (e.g., MobileNetV2) | Train the baseline model twice: once on raw images, once on preprocessed images. Compare training curves. | Accuracy, Validation Loss | Preprocessed dataset yields a faster convergence rate and a 2-5% improvement in baseline classification accuracy. |
| **Objective 2:** Investigate augmentation for class imbalance | Utilize geometric transforms (rotations, flips) and selective oversampling of minority classes (df, vasc, mel, bcc, akiec, bkl) to balance against 'nv'. | HAM10000 | Baseline CNN | Train models on datasets with and without class-balanced augmentation. | F1-Score (macro-averaged), Class-specific Recall | Macro-averaged F1-score increases significantly, proving minority class detection improved. |
| **Objective 3:** Compare pre-trained CNN architectures via transfer learning | Implement Transfer Learning (ImageNet weights) on 5 architectures. Freeze base layers, add custom dense classification heads, and fine-tune. | HAM10000 (Preprocessed & Augmented) | ResNet-50, DenseNet-201, VGG-16, MobileNetV2, InceptionV3 | Train all five architectures under identical conditions (epochs, batch size, optimizer) and rank their performance. | Overall Accuracy, AUC-ROC | DenseNet-201 or ResNet-50 achieves the highest overall accuracy, likely exceeding 90%. |
| **Objective 4:** Analyze per-class performance | Generate detailed classification reports for the best model from Objective 3. Formulate a confusion matrix to identify inter-class confusion. | HAM10000 (Test Split) | Best Model from Obj 3 | Execute model predictions on the held-out test set and calculate metrics for each of the 7 classes independently. | Precision, Recall, F1, Sensitivity, Specificity, Confusion Matrix | Clear identification of which lesion pairs (e.g., mel vs. nv) are most difficult for the model to differentiate. |
| **Objective 5:** Assess interpretability using Grad-CAM | Extract gradients from the final convolutional layer of the best model to generate spatial activation heatmaps (Grad-CAM) overlaid on original images. | HAM10000 (Sample Subset) | Best Model from Obj 3 | Pass correct and incorrect predictions through Grad-CAM and visually inspect the heatmaps. | Qualitative (Visual Heatmap overlay) | Heatmaps show the model focuses on the lesion itself, proving decisions are based on relevant features, not artifacts. |
